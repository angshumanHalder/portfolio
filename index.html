Voice of the Customer: from dashboard to product module

Level AI · Frontend owner and lead · React, Redux

Voice of the Customer helps organizations identify recurring themes across customer conversations and inspect the interactions behind those trends.

I owned and delivered the frontend for VoC 1.0 and 1.5, working across pods with the Analytics team responsible for the backend. These releases established the product foundation and informed the next iteration.

For VoC 2.0, I led the frontend effort from planning and design discussions through production. I reworked the frontend data flow so VoC could operate as an independent module instead of a component within a larger dashboard. After launch, I continued improving performance, helped remove substantial legacy code, and mentored a junior engineer working within the new architecture.

Outcomes

  • Established the frontend foundation across three major releases.
  • Helped turn VoC into a standalone, marketable product module.
  • Received positive customer feedback on the modular redesign.
  • Introduced a more scalable frontend structure for future development.
  • Reduced legacy complexity and created a cleaner base for subsequent work.

Leadership Through Ownership

I was promoted to Lead Frontend Engineer in November 2025 after sustained ownership of high-visibility AI product launches, frontend architecture, and cross-team delivery.

Own the product, not just the interface

I have led frontend work across multiple VoC releases, bridging product, design, Analytics, and engineering teams from early planning through production and follow-up improvements.

Balance speed with durability

I delivered the complete Alerts settings UI in two weeks and the interaction-heavy Conversation Tag v3 interface in six weeks. Both remained stable with minimal rework, demonstrating that rapid delivery does not have to create immediate maintenance cost.

Raise the team’s baseline

I have mentored junior engineers, conducted frontend interviews, standardized candidate evaluation, documented the frontend development lifecycle, and initiated legacy-code cleanup where no clear owner existed.

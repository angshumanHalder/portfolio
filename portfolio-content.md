## Selected Work

The following work was completed for B2B products. Customer identities, internal metrics, confidential implementation details, and product visuals have been omitted or generalized.

### Voice of the Customer: from dashboard to product module

**Level AI · Frontend owner and lead · React, Redux**

Voice of the Customer helps organizations identify recurring themes across customer conversations and inspect the interactions behind those trends.

I owned and delivered the frontend for VoC 1.0 and 1.5, working across pods with the Analytics team responsible for the backend. These releases established the product foundation and informed the next iteration.

For VoC 2.0, I led the frontend effort from planning and design discussions through production. I reworked the frontend data flow so VoC could operate as an independent module instead of a component within a larger dashboard. After launch, I continued improving performance, helped remove substantial legacy code, and mentored a junior engineer working within the new architecture.

**Outcomes**

- Established the frontend foundation across three major releases.
- Helped turn VoC into a standalone, marketable product module.
- Received positive customer feedback on the modular redesign.
- Introduced a more scalable frontend structure for future development.
- Reduced legacy complexity and created a cleaner base for subsequent work.

### Modernizing logistics operations

**Janio Asia · Software Engineer · React ecosystem**

I helped modernize a legacy warehouse scanner interface around clearer operational workflows and built the UI for security-tag tracking used to record signs of cargo tampering.

Across the wider operations portal, I delivered interfaces for postal-code and zone allocation, merchant profiles, and SLA management.

**Outcome:** Replaced legacy experiences and brought several logistics workflows into focused operational interfaces.

### Connecting retail analytics to promotion workflows

**Impact Analytics · Senior Full-stack Engineer · React, Django, PostgreSQL**

I built a data-rich promotion-planning dashboard using historical retail data and developed APIs connecting the product to visualization services owned by the data-science team.

I also implemented role-based dashboard access and APIs supporting promotion creation and approval.

**Outcome:** Connected analytics, permissions, and operational workflows in one decision-support product.
